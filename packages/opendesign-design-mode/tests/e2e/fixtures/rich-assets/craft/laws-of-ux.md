# Laws of UX craft rules

Apply these laws when arranging screens:

- **Hick**: more choices cost decision time — group and prune before adding.
- **Fitts**: primary actions are large and near the cursor's resting zone.
- **Jakob**: reuse conventions your users already know from other products.
- **Miller**: chunk working memory loads to at most 4±1 grouped items.
- **Peak-end**: the last screen of a flow deserves the most polish.
